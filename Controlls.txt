
ACORN ENGINE CONTROLS

PLAYER 1
W / S          Move forward / backward
A / D          Strafe left / right
E / Q          Move locally up / down
F / H          Look left / right
T / G          Look up / down

PLAYER 2
I / K          Move forward / backward
J / L          Strafe left / right
O / U          Move locally up / down
Left / Right   Look left / right
Up / Down      Look up / down

SCENES
1              Load Basic Scene 1
2              Load Basic Scene 2
3              Load Basic Scene 3
4              Load Autumn Forest
5              Load Teapot Space
6              Load Basalt Gallery

Loading a scene resets both players to its starting poses.
Reach the goal to advance to the next scene.
The final scene wraps back to the first.
The green teapot is the goal in Teapot Space.

DISPLAY AND MULTIPLAYER
M              Toggle player 2 and split-screen multiplayer
0              Toggle the scrolling carousel effect
B              Toggle player 1's background color
Tab            Toggle wireframe
Caps Lock      Toggle face culling
Enter          Toggle player 1's perspective/orthographic view

SAVE AND EXIT
F5             Overwrite the selected scene file with its loaded description    Note: F5 is Disabled 
Escape         Quit

NOTES
Movement follows the player's orientation.
Players can turn upside down.
Each player sees the other player's body, but not their own.
Disabling multiplayer pauses and hides player 2.
F5 does not save the players' current positions.
Saved files use the application's working-directory scenes folder.
